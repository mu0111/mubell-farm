## Welcome to MuBell Farm

MuBell Farm is a working farm with a vibrant community at Sandholmgaard in Snekkersten, Denmark. We live close to nature with Icelandic horses, Lagotto Romagnolo dogs, cats, chickens, fruit trees and flowers.

We are still in the early stages, but our vision is clear: a place where people, animals and nature thrive together. We have planted fruit trees, grow flowers, and dream of pick-your-own and farm sales. Our guests are mostly local families and horse enthusiasts.

*It is not about high production. It is about having your feet in the soil.*

## The Household

Sandholmgaard buzzes with life. Children play in the yard, horses graze in the fields, kittens sleep in the hay, and the chickens roam freely. There is always something moving here.

The farm has a long history — the four-winged thatched farm has been standing since 1789 and bears the marks of the many generations who have lived and worked here.
