We offer boarding for Icelandic horses with a focus on animal welfare and natural conditions. The horses have access to large paddocks, shelters and a round pen. The forest is close by, and the water is not far away.

Bellis offers lessons and training for both riders and horses. We are working towards having a riding arena soon, which will improve the facilities even further.

Contact us for prices and availability. We are happy to discuss your needs.
