The Lagotto Romagnolo is an ancient Italian breed, originally used for waterfowl hunting and later for truffle searching. At Sandholmgaard they are an indispensable part of daily life — intelligent, loyal and full of energy.

The breed is known for its exceptional nose, its eagerness to work and its loving temperament. The Lagotto thrives with tasks and activity, but is equally happy to curl up close to the family in the evening.

## Our Lagotto

Our Lagotto Romagnolo dogs are bred with a focus on health, temperament and the breed's original qualities. They live close to people and animals and are a natural part of life on the farm.

*The Lagotto is not just a dog — it is a friend for life.*
