Sandholmgaard is a four-winged thatched farm situated in Snekkersten on the shores of the Øresund. The farm has roots going back to 1789 and bears the mark of the many generations who have lived and worked here.

Over the centuries the farm has housed farmers, craftsmen and families, all of whom have contributed to its character and soul. The walls hold stories of hard work, the turning of the seasons and the importance of community.

## The Character of the Farm

The four-winged farm is a classic Danish farmstead enclosing a central courtyard. The thatched roof is a living testament to the craftsmanship of the era and the respect for materials and traditions that characterised building at that time.

Today the farm lives on in a new age  -  with a co-living community, horses, dogs, chickens and a dream of preserving the best of the past and bringing it into the future.

## From Generation to Generation

MuBell Farm continues with the conviction that the place carries something rare  -  a soul shaped over many lives and many seasons. We take care of it.

*A farm is never finished. It grows with those who live there.*
